In my words

Why me

What I love about Cursor, Grok Bot and Grok Build

The interconnectivity between the three services stunned me, and it only keeps improving. Today I have my setup with Grok Bot at the center, easy to pick up cloud agents that keep working while I'm offline.

On Cursor, I love the versatility of being able to switch between agent mode and IDE. I also like the new Projects feature and am planning to replace my OpenClaw that I've been working on since January/February with Cursor Agents.

And Grok Build is great for daily use to make landing pages, dashboards, and general visualization of anything I do with Bot, and enables me to make anything quick I imagine in a very fast way.

For example, yesterday I thought of making a searchable archive of my 50,000 X bookmarks. Bot used the X API, and I had what I had imagined only a few minutes later.

What I use them for

Today I'm my own gaming and animation studio, producing in the culture/entertainment layer to captivate hearts and minds to the techno optimism cause. And I implement AI/SI for companies through my consultancy firm. I onboarded dozens of tech and non-tech founders on Bot and I just love to see them use it and get awed.

I myself run a fleet of specialized bots (an X growth coach, a nightly check-in that drafts posts in my voice, a backlog bot that tracks my stalled projects, a bookmark archiver, a CRM for my AI consultancy agency) and have it connected to my OpenClaw-based agent team (agent harness and AI chief of staff called Klaus, soon to be Cursor Agents-based).
